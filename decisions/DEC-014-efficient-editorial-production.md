- User request: routine edition creation used too much of the available ChatGPT allowance. Implement the approved plan to reduce usage while improving quality: concise instructions, reusable research records, change-focused editions, routine collection/publishing, suitable licensed-image reuse and separate maintenance.
- Extends DEC-004–013. Explicitly replaces repeated startup document reads, compulsory hidden favourite-sports research, retyping unchanged records and blanket per-slot research of unchanged long-lived opportunities. Keep all three existing schedules/settings, coverage/reading/photo/privacy/source/UI rules and exact two-site publication gates. Since-last-visit tracking remains deferred.
- Status: implementation/replay under review; main/Pages and actual task updates are separately reported in release notes. A replay is not fresh research or measured quota.

## Why

The observed task prompts were roughly 24,000 characters each and directed readers to 242,448 bytes of public startup manuals/schemas, plus other repo instructions and prior editions. The 15 October evening source contains seven articles, 38 market/rate entries and five opportunities whose substantive terms did not change from midday. Its publication also included photo-cache follow-ups and a browser-test change. These show avoidable work and mixed responsibilities, not a measured attribution of Pro quota.

## Concrete workflow

BRIEFING_RUNTIME.md and short slot prompts consolidate the active contract. Short role/operations/spec/handoff files point to it; exact prior public instructions/schema bytes and source commit are archived under maintenance/history. Do not erase earlier rationale or treat archived instructions as another runtime checklist. Private remains a separate repo with its own confidentiality rules.

research.py builds a public-only continuity index and compact private per-run packet from dated published findings, exact IDs/aliases, readable evidence, unresolved notes, due items and Wire health. It excludes the target/later slots and future evidence; retained context is not fresh verification. An explicit source-hashed private checkpoint resumes unfinished research. A mechanical draft scaffold copies dated market/opportunity structures and clears old reviews/overviews/weather rather than generating news or retyping stable records.

The versioned due policy checks standard opportunities daily, enacted rights/closed watchlists weekly and near-date/uncertain/open budget-sensitive items urgently. Actual changed/new official announcements trigger earlier review. Future publications cannot carry overdue active terms as current; honestly uncertain items retain their status/verification provenance. Weather remains a per-slot check. Always perform a short broad news scan so tracked-story reuse does not hide major new events.

collect.py performs bounded HTTPS public-source fetches into private caches, detects relevant content changes and parses conservative quote candidates. It does not write articles, invent completed closes/daily baselines or advance verification times. Explicit quote application is confined to matching drafts, checks units/times/non-older evidence and validates schema. Unsupported/changed/new/failed sources require review. Image reuse similarly requires explicit relevance/original-date/archive approval and exact checked bytes, preserving original attribution/licence/retrieval.

The publishing coordinator adds a blocking checked stage. Content-only checks validate the candidate and affected edition/day/readers with native mobile/desktop/no-JS behavior. Code/schema/template/test/workflow/runtime changes force the full profile; tests cannot be weakened or changed within routine production to rescue an edition. Check fingerprints and release receipts include QA inputs, avoiding stale passes. Failures preserve old-good results and produce a concise stage report plus logs. Source and central deployment remain independently verified.

usage.py records private work counts, gate outcomes and optional actual account-window observations; it never derives Pro cost from tokens/bytes/time. Saved-edition replays expose structural work proxies; the real 3–5 comparable edition pilot requires live scheduled runs after deployment. First goal: at least halve routine evening usage; 15–20% is an unproven further target, not a promised cap. No paid AI/API migration, model change, new scheduler, new message destination or unverified edition is authorised.
