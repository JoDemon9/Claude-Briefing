## Implemented behavior

- A compact active runtime and short role/operations/spec/handoff files replace repeated manual/schema/renderer reads. Prior instructions and exact baseline commit remain archived.
- Research packets keep dated findings/IDs/evidence and explicit private unresolved questions/due checks, with same-source checkpoint resume. A draft scaffold copies stable quote/opportunity structures without retyping or carrying false review/weather/overview claims.
- Versioned due policies retain real verified_at and source times. Broad discovery remains mandatory; stale Wire falls back to direct scanning. New publications cannot carry overdue active terms as current.
- Bounded public-source collection and conditional caches do not write news or advance verification times. Quote candidates require explicit matching-draft application, identity/unit/time checks and schema validation; close/baseline ambiguities remain explicit.
- Photo reuse requires a real decoded licensed source, exact checksums and explicit relevance/original-date/archive review. Original licence/attribution/retrieval remains unchanged.
- The publisher has a blocking checked stage. Content-only changes run the source gates and affected edition/day/readers at mobile/desktop with JavaScript on/off. Publisher/schema/template/test/workflow/runtime changes force the complete regression profile. Failures stop at a named stage and keep logs.
- Private work/gate ledgers accept optional actual Pro-window observations and never turn work/time/bytes into quota cost. No paid AI/API, model switch, schedule change or new scheduler is introduced.
