# Briefing specification

This is the canonical editorial and data contract. Execution budgets belong to [CHATGPT_TASK_INSTRUCTIONS.md](CHATGPT_TASK_INSTRUCTIONS.md); exact fields belong to [content.schema.json](templates/v2/content.schema.json). Historical records are rationale, not additional instructions.

## Product and selection

Publish clear Greek in independent morning_scan, midday_delta and evening_synthesis snapshots. The cumulative newspaper includes useful selected coverage from the whole available day, not merely the latest slot's summary. Its overview cites actual participating story IDs; preserve independent archived readers and original times.

Target **4–5 supported Cyprus and 4–5 international stories separately**. Count the lead in its actual geographic desk; Sports does not fill a news target. Broad evidence discovery happens in Python before synthesis. Read every visible candidate; prefer consequential distinct events over recency, repeated election reports, promotional shows or cartoon columns. Reserve news evidence before optional authored Sports or extra corroboration. No filler or fabricated shortage research.

For new `sports.briefing_policy=intelligence-v1` editions, the bounded profile reserves up to 10 authored news records in Morning and 6 changed news records later, plus up to three wider-sports briefs and four short favourite annotations. Older sealed packs retain their shared 10/6 allowance. Later same-day carries can preserve complete coverage. The current policy allows at most 5 Cyprus and 7 world stories; 6–7 world stories require an explicit editorial reason. Historical accepted editions retain their original contracts.

For new `selection_policy=balanced-v1` candidates, fewer than four stories in either news desk requires a specific `coverage_notes` reason for that desk. This is an explanation of actual evidence/selection limits, not a claim of extra research. A small supported edition may publish with that explanation; an unexplained coverage collapse fails validation. The threshold is four, the preferred range is four to five. CI reports actual counts, carried counts and shortfall reasons. New sealed packs select this policy; standalone compilation and direct current-contract editions from 2026-10-28 must use it too. Already sealed older packs retain their policy until that cutoff. Editions accepted before deployment retain their exact historical contracts/fingerprints even if deployment happens after the cutoff; edited or unaccepted records cannot claim that exception.

## Evidence and useful depth

Read evidence for every new/material claim. Record publisher, HTTPS URL, original publication time (null if unknown), actual retrieval time, reporting group, kind, access and claim use. Feed-only items, HTTP success and metadata are discovery, not proof of factual review. Independently corroborate consequential/disputed claims where needed within the budget, or narrow/omit them. Never invent sources, outcomes, licences or fresh checks.

Give each desk a substantive lead when evidence supports it: what changed, mechanism/incentive, who is affected, uncertainty and an observable next test. Aim for 120–170 Greek words across its analytical fields, not a quota. Analytical fields require `analysis_sources` drawn from read evidence. Scenarios are conditional paths, not facts or arbitrary probabilities. New `briefing_policy=depth-markets-v1` packs use ordinary secondary news summaries of usually three sentences and 55–75 Greek words: what happened, the decisive number/decision, who is affected and timing. An optional distinct, sourced consequence is 15–25 words; summary plus consequence stays within 90 words. Weak evidence has no word minimum. Older packs retain two factual sentences and the 70-word ceiling. Sports retains its separate word limits. Extra depth is reserved for desk leads and supported market/tactical movers. No compulsory essay, counterargument or decorative scenario. A full reader must add distinct knowledge; otherwise link the readable source and retain the dated archive route.

Executive synthesis has 1–3 points, at most 160 words total (aim 90–120), connecting supported developments to implications and a next test. Cite selected/carried story IDs; unchanged risks are dated context, never fresh verification. Do not invent connections or event history.

## Identity, lead and continuity

Semantic ASCII slugs are valid filenames. No section prefix or separate `lead.json` is required. Author `section=cyprus|world|sports` independently of `is_lead`; canonical JSON represents the lead through `daily.lead_story_id` and the lead category. The selected lead ID must resolve to one of the edition's articles. If no explicit lead survives, the compiler selects the first supported article. A desk's first changed article is its analytical lead, independent of the edition lead.

Use stable `story_id` and `story_identity` for one evolving event. Explicit flat aliases reconcile reviewed identities only; an empty alias map is valid. Never infer identity or follow-ups from similar headlines. `daily.follow_ups` links genuinely related, different published stories; reject self, missing, future or conflicting references. Later-coverage links belong to the cumulative day, never earlier snapshots.

Name an exact earlier baseline edition and fingerprint. Later slots must account for each baseline story by a material update, an explicit exact carry, or an explicit omission with a reason. Carry only useful **same-day** records; never roll yesterday's unchanged copy into today's newspaper. An omission retires it from the later snapshot while preserving earlier coverage in the archive/day. Across days, reselect only with supported new reporting. Preserve original publication/source/image times; copies get no false novelty or fabricated events. UPDATED/REVERSAL/CORRECTION require a real factual delta; identical reporting cannot become an update by changing timestamps, status, headline or ID. UNKNOWN is never automatically UNCHANGED. Original baseline records and hashes remain immutable.

## Markets, opportunities and weather

Keep all 39 instrument identities, including AMZN, exactly once in new depth-markets-v1 editions (accepted historical editions retain the original inventory), with absent quotes null/unavailable. Preserve actual quote/fixing/effective times, currency, unit and session status. Never infer a close, tenor or causal explanation from price movement. Explain supported transmission and practical Cyprus implications ahead of raw numbers; keep full data and one shared source/time disclosure. No trading advice, unsupported targets or confidence percentages.

New packs use financial_policy=news-insights-v1 alongside depth-markets-v1. Python reads financial news every edition: reserve three financial event candidates, up to one additional different reporting chain for confirmation, six Cyprus and four general-world candidates and seven sports lanes within the SAME 24 article requests. Empty places return to other reporting; repairs stay within the cap. Finance includes results/guidance, monetary policy, inflation, sector changes and financially relevant security incidents even without large quote moves. Prefer consequence, readable evidence and reporting diversity; publisher variety is not proof of independence. Company aliases and price alerts assist discovery, never establish event identity or causation. Two marked priority candidates need coverage in an insight or selected news article, or a specific @SkipPriority reason. Distinguish exchange, blockchain, supplier and individual-account incidents, confirmed impact and allegations.

Lead the markets section with two or three DISTINCT article-backed financial insights, each 70–100 Greek words across reported development/explanation, conditional editorial meaning and next observable test; maximum 300 words overall. A short headline is separate. Read relevant financial article passages, connect only supported reported drivers to market behavior, explain an overlooked practical implication and state uncertainty. Figures versus expectations require a source reporting those expectations. A stock underperforming its index does not establish a company-specific cause. Do not manufacture a catalyst, scenario, earnings expectation, breach victim or loss amount. Use @FinanceReason with a specific evidence/novelty limitation when fewer than two insights are supported. A selected news article may supply the same evidence, but its prose should not be repeated. Generic price movements belong in the statistics rather than filling insight slots.

@Insight instruments=AMZN,^IXIC evidence=e1,e2 stats=m1 reported="..." meaning="..." watch="..." | Short Greek headline resolves sources and one optional comparison chart from the sealed pack. instruments=none supports macro/security developments without a tracked company; stats=none honestly omits unavailable comparable data. Each named company requires identifying article evidence. Broad macro reporting need not name every index it affects. @Market remains accepted only for older sealed packs. Same-day @CarryInsight index=1 preserves an exact baseline insight with its original analysis/source/chart times; updates require new reporting, not a new timestamp. Never carry yesterday's insight.

Python derives comparison charts from the already-fetched provider daily-bar responses, without extra requests or model chart-reading. Use matching completed session dates, at least two observations, consistent adjusted/unadjusted bases, truthful start/end dates and percentage-point excess returns. Exclude future/incomplete sessions, stale series and unadjusted known splits. Nasdaq is a broad comparison for the tracked technology companies; S&P 500 for other tracked equities, not a claim that either is a precise sector benchmark. The model sees only compact statistics/aliases; full observations render as accessible inline charts and expandable tables beside the insight. Intra-session and extended-hours quote moves remain separately dated and must not be mixed with these completed-session returns.

Python still flags dated equity moves from 3% (priority from 5%), indices/futures from 1.5% and crypto from 5% to guide discovery. Preserve provider references/session labels and actual quote times. Do not label delayed quotes a close, infer a 24-hour crypto interval or use chart-window baselines as daily returns. Under news-insights-v1 the executive market fallback uses sourced insight reporting and the section no longer auto-promotes percentage-only alerts. Old packs and accepted historical editions retain their declared presentation and 10–65-word @Market contract. All input, output, search, turn and article-request ceilings remain unchanged; reference tokens are not measured account quota.

Opportunities retain stable call IDs, official read sources, `verified_at`, eligibility, support, stage, opening/deadline type/date, budget constraints, application route and next action. Preserve date-only deadlines. Closed/proposed/unknown is not an active entitlement; infer no personal eligibility. Due records retain original verification, become `review_due=true` and lose active application promotion. The day selects the latest actual verification; show three actionable records first and disclose the rest/watchlist.

Python checks weather each slot; preserve real location, forecast validity and warning expiry. A forecast fetch is not an official warning check. Expired/unreviewed data is unavailable or clearly historical. A scheduled/started event does not establish an outcome. Never silently inherit market prices, forecasts, sports checks or opportunity verification from a baseline.

## Sports and photographs

Keep Omonoia, Manchester United, Real Madrid and F1 as compact logo cards. Each supported favourite annotation explains what changed, its sporting consequence and an observable next test in 40–60 words (70 maximum), with reviewed article sources. Missing evidence means unavailable analysis, never an invented “no news” claim. Tactics, injuries and xG require article evidence; scores alone cannot support them. Show the next fixture/session in Cyprus time, with full results, statistics, headlines and sources inside native disclosures. Put UEFA tables after editorial coverage. Preserve full Python data, original check times, historical reporting, logos and licensed assets; provider gaps stay explicit, especially incomplete Omonoia domestic coverage.

Within the 48,000-byte digest, reserve readable evidence for up to five candidates per news desk and one candidate per available sports interest/topic before spending space on alternatives or corroborating copies. A projection overflow must never silently remove sports coverage: shorten excerpts within the supported bounds, omit extra alternatives explicitly, or fail preparation if the reserved evidence cannot fit. Collection failure and input-budget omission are different conditions.

Omonoia discovery also uses Kerkida's current club listing, Goal/Philenews (the destination of goal.com.cy), and OmonoiaNews football reporting. Keep the existing article-request cap, read the underlying report before making claims, distinguish Omonoia Nicosia from Aradippou and 29M, and attribute commentary or club allegations rather than presenting them as independently established facts. Goal shares the Philenews reporting group; multiple URLs do not establish source independence.

Reserve two to three distinct briefs beyond the four favourites: one padel, one general-football and, when consequential, one other major sport. Cover both women's and men's competition; avoid promotional material and duplicate events. Each brief has facts and a distinct, sourced consequence, ideally 35–50 words and at most 70 across those fields. If fewer than two are supported, record the actual evidence limitation. The whole sports overview aims for 300–400 words, maximum 450, within the existing model output-token ceiling. These briefs have a separate allowance and cannot replace Cyprus/world news. Favourite annotations are edition-specific and are never silently inherited from a baseline.

`real-photos-v1` requires a decoded, licensed, relevant real photograph on every article. This intentionally supersedes the old partial-policy missing-photo exception, not other partial-data behavior. Python first extracts source-article photographs from the HTML already fetched for readable evidence. Only publisher-declared photographs with image-level CC BY/CC BY-SA/CC0/public-domain rights, attribution and a usable caption, or exact reviewed image grants, qualify. A page licence, social preview or permitted CDN alone never grants image reuse. Assets and provenance are sealed privately before synthesis; compilation stays offline and attaches only photos belonging to cited evidence. These automated metadata checks are recorded as `source_review`, never a fabricated human visual review. Source photo failure falls back to the reviewed licensed subject library, then clearly labelled broad archive/context photographs. Every article still gets a photo. Never imply a source or context photo depicts the reported event; retain archive wording and original credits. No generated illustration or AI image search. Invalid explicit selections or corrupted sealed assets fail instead of silently substituting another photo.

New uses reference the existing reviewed library asset URL, retaining per-article caption/alt/relevance review and immutable source/author/licence/retrieval metadata. Do not create another slug-specific binary for the same photograph. Preserve existing published image URLs and their exact bytes; no history rewrite to recover disk space. Full rights and captions live on the shared credits page, with concise archive/context labels where needed.

Source photographs use shared content-addressed assets and original retrieval times. Prefer distinct eligible photos across different stories in the edition and cumulative day using exact/visual hashes; unchanged carries retain their exact original image. Relevance takes precedence over variety: reuse a relevant fallback when no suitable alternative exists. Image extraction adds no article-page requests and no model-facing photo payload. Python permits at most 24 bounded image downloads (four workers, one attempt, five-second timeout, 5 MB input/300 KB output each), with a private one-day cache. Existing digest, search, tool-call and synthesis-token budgets remain unchanged; machine work is not measured account quota.

## Availability, reading and release

`partial-v1` keeps a useful edition during item-level market, sports, weather or evidence outages. Omit unsupported claims/articles; explain shortfalls honestly. Block structural/schema/identity/security/integrity failures, invalid mandatory photos, zero supported articles and actual preview/publication failures. Never weaken a gate to label failure as success.

Standard shows complete facts; Full opens useful analysis; Executive uses supplied synthesis and compact market data. Native disclosures, tabs and filters enhance an accessible no-JS/print report. Keep compact mobile edition navigation, photo-first mobile lead, one-row meaningful actions with 44px targets, themes, search, bookmarks and the 3/2/1 grid. No repeated reading-duration labels, dummy actions or since-last-visit tracking.

JSON remains canonical; Markdown is a versioned input adapter into the same compiler. No producer-written HTML/CSS. Preserve `reading_review.version=2`, `image_review.version=2`, fresh capability/schema contracts, unchanged historical snapshots, original source/quote times and evidence provenance. Three daily gates: content (validate/build once), preview (selected edition and cumulative day at 390px with JS and 1440px without JS), integrity (unchanged exact receipt). Maintenance adds units; full archive browser audits are explicit manual work. Verify exact source Pages before portal sync and exact portal bytes afterward; no fake live-verification bypass.
