# Editorial specification

The normative routine contract is [BRIEFING_RUNTIME.md](BRIEFING_RUNTIME.md). It consolidates DEC-004–014 and replaces contradictory historical word counts, sports panels, reading labels and blanket document-reading instructions. The exact JSON schema is templates/v2/content.schema.json; derive compact capabilities with backend.py. Fetch a field/example only when needed; validate the complete data by code.

- Every slot targets five International and five Cyprus stories separately (DEC-017); count the lead in its real section. Maximum five Cyprus/seven world; six/seven world need a documented reason. From 18 October evening require selection_review.version=2 and evidence-backed section-specific shortfalls after broader discovery. Later editions retain useful dated context honestly, add meaningful developments and avoid filler/false novelty.
- Preserve stable story IDs and original times. The cumulative daily overview reviews all participating selected stories with real supporting IDs; independent snapshots remain as-of. No inferred/fuzzy merges or invented event history.
- Findings normally 60–90 Greek words, maximum 110; optional specific supported analysis normally 80–140, maximum 180. Full readers only add distinct evidence/value; otherwise link the readable original with a dated archival record. No minimum essay length or forced counterargument.
- Read evidence for new/materially changed claims; independently corroborate consequential/disputed claims where it matters. Record actual access, source/retrieval times and reporting chain. Neither HTTP 200 nor a snippet/cache proves factual review.
- Every article has a real relevant licensed decoded local photograph. Reuse needs explicit relevance/archive-date review and immutable rights/source provenance; no generated news image or unsupported licence. Keep complete source metadata/alt and shared credits.
- Four favourites are small authentic-logo/official-news links only; no hidden research panels. Up to two other-sports stories exclude all four interests. Retain old coverage records honestly for compatibility.
- Preserve full market/rate data with actual quote/fixing/effective times. Analyse core instruments/material movers; never infer causes, session closes, rate tenors or fresh prices. Deterministic collection/quote candidates do not establish editorial review.
- Opportunities preserve verified_at and full official eligibility/application/deadline/status data. Due reviews follow status, budget sensitivity and dates; carry unchanged records honestly. Closed/proposed/unknown is not an active entitlement, and no personal eligibility is inferred.
- Weather is checked every slot, with true location/validity/warning expiry. Current freshness audits do not rewrite historical snapshots or infer event results.
- Native mobile navigation, complete summaries, photo-first mobile lead, one-row actions, optional analyses/sources, shared photo credits, genuine later-coverage links and archived readers remain. No repeated reading labels or since-last-visit tracking.

The pre-consolidation specification is in maintenance/history/2026-10-15/BRIEFING_SPEC.md for maintenance reference only. Current instructions and explicit user requests take precedence.
