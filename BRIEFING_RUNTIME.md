## Selection and reading

Select before writing. Make one bounded discovery pass across at least two independent broad world-news desks, not just a Cyprus outlet, an old tracked list or market headlines. Consider conflict/humanitarian developments, major government decisions, the economy, public health/disasters and consequential science/technology when there is real news. Privately keep a short ranked shortlist with a one-line inclusion/omission reason; investigate the selected stories deeply rather than every candidate. Rank actual human impact, scale, urgency and genuinely changed facts above routine announcements, local colour or statements of intent. Check the shortlist once for an obvious major omission. A thin discovery pass is not evidence of a quiet day. Midday/evening must scan outside the morning list and add missed important coverage honestly as a later addition, never backdate it.

Every slot: select five International and five Cyprus stories separately, counting the lead once in its actual daily.section. Prioritise major world news. Five Cyprus/seven world are ceilings; six/seven world need a specific global_exception_reason. Select before writing and broaden discovery if either section is short. Do not silently turn five per section into five combined. Up to two independently worthwhile other-sports articles are separate and do not fill either section. Midday/evening add meaningful developments and missed major news, retaining useful earlier coverage with original article/source times and honest dated context; never call unchanged coverage new. New slot article records keep stable story identities and original published_at; updated_at changes only for real editorial changes. The cumulative day keeps one selected version per story. No relabelling, duplicate stories or padding.

DEC-017 starts with 18 October evening, leaving completed snapshots unchanged. Every new published slot needs selection_review.version=2, completed_at, global_exception_reason (normally null), and section_shortfalls (normally {}). If broader research genuinely cannot support five worthwhile stories, record only the affected world/cyprus key with a specific reason (at least 40 characters) and checked_sources from at least two independent HTTPS source domains. Sources must really have been checked; different domains alone do not prove independence. Explain the actual evidence/source/photo limitation rather than generic quiet-day prose. Investigate missing major coverage first. Remove an exception after its section reaches five. An unexplained shortage blocks release. CI reports section counts/targets/shortfalls.

Each article has a self-contained factual summary, normally 60–90 Greek words, at most 110. Include who/what/when, material figures and qualifications changing the meaning. Optional newspaper_analysis normally 80–140 words, at most 180, adds specific sourced context/comparison/mechanism/consequence. Omit filler, invented causal explanations, generic caveats and mandatory hypothetical counterarguments. A real material disagreement/denial remains. Analysis sources must be readable, used-for-claims article sources. reader_mode=full only with distinct substantiated added value recorded in reader_value; otherwise source with a concise dated archive and readable original link. No minimum essay length. Avoid copying publisher prose. Set reading_review.version=2 and image_review.version=2 with actual completion times after review.

Write clear natural Greek: lead with what happened, then explain the concrete consequence and any genuinely useful next step. Keep material attribution, contested facts and uncertainty beside the affected claim. Do not fill summaries or analysis with research-process narration, lists of what was not proved, generic cautions or a forced Cyprus angle. Remove an analysis that only repeats the summary or explains obvious distinctions. Read the finished edition once as a reader: each headline must identify the news and each expansion must add useful knowledge. Optional analysis may be empty; the photograph must still render.

Read the current source supporting each new/materially changed claim. For consequential/disputed stories seek the original document and meaningful independent corroboration; syndicated copies are one chain. An authoritative primary release does not need an unrelated second link. A snippet, paywall, inaccessible page or HTTP 200 does not establish that reporting was read. Preserve source publication/retrieval times, publisher, reporting_group, kind/access and evidence limits. Unknowns stay unknown/null. No invented facts, quotes, forecasts, licences, results or novelty. Private financial/employment/personal details never appear publicly; calculators/examples are generic.
